The Situation

PayFlow processes over $2.4 Billion in cross-border settlements annually. Rapid growth resulted in database lock contention and race conditions on their primary Postgres clusters during regional market opens.

"Our senior engineering team was spending nearly half their sprint bandwidth dealing with database lock contention and sync failures during market opens."
— Marcus Vance, VP of Infrastructure at PayFlow
2

The Task & Technical Criteria

PayFlow needed an enterprise-ready distributed state layer that satisfied four strict engineering criteria:

  • Sub-12ms global P99 latency
  • SOC2 Type II compliance
  • Zero data loss (RPO = 0)
  • Phased shadow traffic deployment
3

Action & Phased Migration

Using DeepSeek Nexus TypeScript SDK, PayFlow executed a 3-stage migration plan over 11 business days.

Days 1-3: VPC Peering setup and SDK integration in sandbox environment.
Days 4-7: Shadow traffic replication at 60,000 req/sec to verify state.
Days 8-11: Live DNS cutover with zero downtime.
4

Results & Long-Term Metrics

By replacing legacy queuing infrastructure with DeepSeek, PayFlow reduced overall compute expenses by $340,000 annually while scaling transaction throughput 4x.
